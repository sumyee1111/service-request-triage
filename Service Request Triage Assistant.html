Service Request Triage Assistant

Paste a customer email. The page compares a keyword-rules baseline with an AI reading of the same email, then recommends category, priority, SLA and whether a human should handle it.

Demo built on synthetic data. Companies, people and emails are fictional. SLA = first-response target: P1 1 hour, P2 4 hours, P3 1 working day. AI suggestions are drafts and always need human review before sending.
